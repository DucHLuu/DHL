About Me

Hello, my name is Duc (Hoang) Luu (or Luu Hoang Duc in Vietnamese order). I am a mathematician working in the fields of random dynamical systems, stochastic analysis, stochastic numerics, and related areas such as financial mathematics, neuro-science, machine learning.

Research Interests

  • Stochastic differential equations, rough path theory, neural (controlled) differential equations, data driven dynamical systems.
  • Stochastic dynamics: random/nonautonomous dynamical systems, stochastic stability, random / nonautonomous (numerical) attractors, perturbation theory, spectral theory, ergodicity.
  • Finite-time dynamics: Lagrangian coherent structures, finite time Lyapunov exponents, finite time entropy.
  • Stochastic finance: stochastic volatility models, interest rate models, rough path lifts and signatures in price models with order book, arbitrage theory.
  • Neuro-science: stochastic FitzHugh-Nagumo neuron model, leaky integrate-and-fire model, stochastic resonance, action potential dynamics.
  • Data science: signature method for datastream, learning stable dynamic structures, stochastic stability of deep Markov models, Lyapunov neural networks, diffusion model, flow matching and score matching.
