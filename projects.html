Rough paths and random dynamics

This project collects all research papers and lecture notes on the topics of rough path theory, rough differential equations and their dynamics. The asymptotic dynamics is then studied under the framework of random/nonautonomous dynamical systems, namely the existence and upper semi-continuity of the global random/nonautonomous pullback attractor, as well as its numeric approximation.

Data driven dynamical systems

The aim of the project is to study aspects in stochastic numerics of data driven dynamical systems via the tools of rough path theory and stochastic analysis, in particular to pose challenging problems in application to data science, neuro-science and machine learning. The motivation behind this research proposal lies in the transformative potential of integrating deep learning and differential equations. This interdisciplinary approach enables us to tackle previously unsolvable problems, particularly those involving dynamic and time-varying phenomena. Whether in understanding the behavior of biological systems, optimizing industrial processes, or simulating complex physical interactions, the synergy between deep learning and differential equations opens the door to a wide array of applications with profound implications.

Modelling stock prices

Empirical studies show that stock prices should have long memory, while the volatility is rough and follows a short-memory process of Hurst exponent 0.1. There are stylized facts that addressed by Mandelbrot, Clark and others that the log-return should not follows normal distribution but should be modeled as a multi-fractal process with stable distribution. Recent theoretical studies with financial data suggest a rough differential equation model, that is arbitrage free under the assumption of transaction costs which is a consequence of the conditional full support (CFS).
